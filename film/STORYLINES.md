# AgentShelf launch film: three storylines

**The one thing:** AI shopping assistants read products and can't see them, so AgentShelf makes every fact legible, with a receipt.

**What the viewer should remember:** "If it isn't written down, the AI doesn't know my sofa exists."

**The spine:** one shopper's question travels through the whole film: *"Stain-resistant 3-seater under ₹60,000, good with a dog?"*

**Assumptions:**
- Length: 30–40 s.
- Format: 16:9 at 1920×1080, 30 fps. A 1:1 cut for LinkedIn can come later.
- Audio: no voiceover. Music bed plus a few effects.
- Cost: no paid items. No avatar, no paid voice. Music is either your own royalty-free track or the HeyGen catalog if you sign in; otherwise the film ships silent.
- The brand ("Arden") is fictional. Every product claim on screen comes from the site and the engine's real output.

---

## 1 (recommended): Invisible until it's written

*It acts out the headline, so a stranger gets it with the sound off.*

- **Device and form:** the object is the explanation, carried as a chain of objects in one home showroom. A beautiful sofa sits in a showroom. When the AI's scan passes over it, the sofa **disappears** and only a faint outline is left, because nothing about it is written down. Then each fact that gets written, quoted and approved makes one part of the sofa physically come back.
- **First 2 seconds:** a warm, lit sofa in a calm showroom. A shopper's question card slides in beside it. A thin scan line sweeps across, and the sofa dissolves into a dashed outline. *(Frame 0 is the lit sofa, which is a good thumbnail.)*
- **Beats:**
  1. **Hook:** the sofa vanishes under the AI's scan.
  2. **Tension:** an AI answer card lists three other sofas. "Arden" isn't there.
  3. **Reveal:** "Make it legible", with the AgentShelf mark.
  4. **Proof, using the engine's real output:**
     - the `agentshelf audit` command runs;
     - a quote is highlighted and marked ✓ "found on page", and the frame turns solid sheesham wood;
     - "performance velvet" is highlighted, and the fabric appears;
     - a made-up "10-year warranty" is crossed out ("dropped: quote not on page"). This is real engine behaviour;
     - an approval sheet row reads "Y";
     - the feed shows `<g:product_detail>` lines.
  5. **Payoff:** the full sofa is back. The camera pulls back to the AI answer card, which now recommends Arden with citations.
  6. **Claim:** "Every fact, with a receipt."
  7. **End card:** the shelf mark builds block by block, then the wordmark and "Get a free AI shelf snapshot".
- **Look:** a linen showroom that turns into a drawing-sheet grid when the AI looks. Evergreen line-work, marker-yellow highlights only on quotes, set in Schibsted Grotesk.
- **Sound:** a warm, sparse piano-and-pulse bed at about 96 bpm. It drops to near silence when the sofa vanishes and opens up again at the payoff. A soft marker-squeak on each highlight, a quiet pop on each verified fact, one riser into the full sofa.

## 2: The spec sheet, in one take

- **Device and form:** one real piece of data travels from page to drawing to answer, filmed as one continuous take across a single drawing sheet. The camera never cuts. It travels from a product page, along connector lines, into a technical drawing of the sofa, and then out to the AI's answer, so you watch facts flow from page to drawing to recommendation.
- **First 2 seconds:** an extreme close-up of the words "stain-resistant performance velvet" on a product page. A marker stroke sweeps across them and the camera starts to pull back.
- **Beats:**
  1. **Hook:** the highlight sweeps.
  2. **Tension:** the pull-back reveals the drawing is all "?".
  3. **Reveal:** the title lands on the drawing sheet.
  4. **Proof:** the camera rides each connector line into a callout: width, frame, warranty. The real CLI output and approval row appear as stops along the way.
  5. **Payoff:** the camera rises over the finished drawing to the AI answer card.
  6. **Claim.**
  7. **End card.**
- **Look:** the website's hero world at film scale. Grid paper, evergreen ink, marker yellow.
- **Sound:** a steady minimal pulse at about 110 bpm. A soft tick on each callout landing and one riser at the answer.

## 3 (the bold one): Two readers

- **Device and form:** life before and after, told as split screen collapsing to one. On the left, a shopper scrolls a gorgeous listing (photo, swatches, "Luxuriously soft"). On the right, the same seconds are seen by the AI as plain text, with "[image: no readable text]" and "unknown" piling up. The two sides fall further out of sync. AgentShelf fills in the AI side fact by fact, the panes merge, and both readers arrive at the same answer: this sofa.
- **First 2 seconds:** a split frame. A smooth, beautiful scroll on the left; the same page as stuttering grey text on the right. A label under each: "What you see" and "What AI reads".
- **Beats:**
  1. **Hook:** the split.
  2. **Tension:** the right side fills with "unknown".
  3. **Reveal:** the AgentShelf mark wipes across the divider.
  4. **Proof:** each fact crosses from left to right with its quote. The real CLI and approval row appear.
  5. **Payoff:** the panes merge into one frame with the AI recommending the sofa.
  6. **Claim.**
  7. **End card.**
- **Look:** a warm lifestyle palette on the left and a cool drawing-sheet on the right, converging into one evergreen-and-linen frame.
- **Sound:** two layers. A lush bed on the left and a dry ticking on the right that resolve into one track at the merge.
