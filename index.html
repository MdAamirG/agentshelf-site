When shoppers ask AI for a sofa, does it know yours?

AI shopping assistants recommend products using facts they can read: dimensions, fabric, warranty. Most product pages keep those facts in images and PDFs. We find them, cite every one to its source, and publish them where Google and ChatGPT look.

Your best facts are where AI can't read them

Assistants like Google AI Mode and ChatGPT compare products on written, structured facts. When the answer is only on a size chart image or in a PDF, your sofa drops out of the comparison, even when it's the better fit.

Dimensions in a picture

A beautiful dimension chart is invisible to a model reading your page text and product feed.

Specs in a download

Frame wood, foam density and fabric rub count sit in a spec-sheet PDF that never reaches Merchant Center.

Adjectives instead of answers

“Luxurious comfort” doesn't answer “is it good with a dog?” A stated fabric and cleaning method does.

How it works

  1. See your shelf

    We ask AI assistants the questions your shoppers ask, many times over, and show you how often you appear next to the brands you compete with.

  2. Find the facts

    We read your product pages, images and spec sheets and pull out the facts assistants compare on. Each one comes with the exact words it came from.

  3. You approve

    Your team reviews every fact in a simple sheet: approve, correct or reject. Claims about materials, stains, pets and warranty get extra care.

  4. Publish and measure

    We hand your team or agency a Merchant Center feed and product markup to upload, then re-run the same questions to show what changed.

Every fact has a receipt

A wrong “stain-resistant” claim can get a product disapproved on Google. So nothing we extract is trusted on its own word.

What we won't promise

Nobody can guarantee a spot in an AI answer, and anyone who does is guessing. Answers change from one question to the next, so we never judge your shelf by a single screenshot. We run every question several times and report the range.

What we can promise: your products will be described completely and accurately, in the places assistants read, with a source for every claim.

Questions brands ask

Does this work in India?

Yes, for Google: AI Mode and Gemini read product data from Google Merchant Center, and that is live in India. ChatGPT's shopping results are currently US-focused, so for Indian brands we only promise Google.

Will ChatGPT or Google definitely recommend us after this?

No one can promise that. We make sure your products are described completely and accurately where assistants look, and we measure your shelf before and after with the same questions.

What if you extract a fact wrong?

Every fact carries the exact sentence it came from, and nothing is published until your team approves it. If a quote isn't on your page word for word, the fact is dropped before you ever see it.

Do you need access to our store or Merchant Center?

No. We work from your public pages and hand the feed and markup to your team or agency to upload. We never ask for passwords.

We already work with an SEO agency.

Good. This is product data work, not content writing, and it fits alongside them. Many of our conversations start with the agency.
