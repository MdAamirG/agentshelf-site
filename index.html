Questions founders ask

Does this work in India?

Yes. Google’s AI Mode and Gemini read product data from Google Merchant Center, and that is live in India. ChatGPT’s shopping results are US-focused for now, so for Indian brands we focus on Google.

What if a fact comes out wrong?

Every fact carries the exact sentence it came from, and nothing is delivered until your team approves it. If the words aren’t on your page, the fact is dropped before you ever see it.

We already have an SEO agency.

Good. This is product data, not content writing, and it fits alongside their work. Many of our conversations start with the agency.

Does it work with Shopify?

Yes, and with WooCommerce or a custom site. The output is a standard Merchant Center feed and product markup, which every platform accepts.

How soon will we see a difference?

Google takes a few weeks to pick up new feed data, so we check again about four weeks after your upload, asking the same questions as before.
